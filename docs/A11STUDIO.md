# **A11STUDIO**

## **What is A11STUDIO?**

A11STUDIO combines the best parts of a professional portfolio website with the simplicity and engagement of social media.

Traditional websites give artists complete control over how they present their work, while social media makes publishing content effortless and helps people build communities. A11STUDIO was created to bring these two worlds together.

Instead of forcing artists to choose between a beautiful portfolio or an easy-to-use platform, A11STUDIO offers both.

### **What a traditional website offers**

* A professional online presence.  
* A portfolio that strengthens your personal brand.  
* Organized projects and galleries.  
* Multiple sections to showcase different types of work.  
* Better visibility on search engines.  
* A permanent home for your art that you own.

### **What social media offers**

* Extremely easy content publishing.  
* Fast profile creation.  
* No coding knowledge required.  
* Minimal technical configuration.  
* Dynamic content updates.  
* The ability to build a community around your work.

### **Where traditional websites fall short**

Although websites are powerful, they usually require:

* Technical knowledge.  
* SEO optimization.  
* Hosting and maintenance.  
* Time to configure.  
* Hiring a developer or learning web development.

Many artists simply want to create art—not spend hours learning technology.

### **The A11STUDIO approach**

A11STUDIO removes the technical barriers.

Artists can build a professional portfolio within minutes while benefiting from features usually only available through custom-built websites.

The platform handles the difficult parts automatically, allowing artists to focus entirely on their creative work.

# **About the Creator**

Hi, I'm Thiago.

I'm a software engineer, but before anything else, I'm an artist.

Photography and filmmaking have always been my greatest passions, although I deeply appreciate every form of art.

Years ago, I built a YouTube channel focused on photography and travel that grew to more than 50,000 subscribers. While reaching that audience was exciting, it also came with an unexpected cost.

Over time, I felt that I was creating for algorithms instead of creating for myself.

In 2021, after experiencing burnout, I stepped away from photography and filmmaking entirely. I no longer enjoyed making art because it felt like I was producing content for social media rather than expressing my creativity.

Leaving photography created an empty space in my life.

I've always been someone who loves learning and building things, so I started exploring new interests. After trying several hobbies, programming caught my attention. Although I had always enjoyed computers, I had never considered software development seriously.

I took a small Python course and built my first simple application—a program that guessed a user's age.

That was enough.

I completely fell in love with programming.

Since then, I've been obsessed with learning everything I can about software engineering.

In 2023, I first imagined building something for photographers and artists. The idea never left me. Even while creating other projects over the following years, there was always a voice reminding me that I wanted to build something meaningful for the creative community.

Finally, in the summer of 2025, I started A11STUDIO.

Since that day, I haven't stopped working on it.

Building A11STUDIO has been one of the most fulfilling experiences of my life because it combines everything I love:

* Art  
* Design  
* Photography  
* Filmmaking  
* Programming  
* Building products that genuinely help people

A11STUDIO isn't just another project.

It's the platform I always wished had existed when I was an artist trying to share my work with the world.
