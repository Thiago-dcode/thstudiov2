# Growth playbook: keywords, authority, links

Read when choosing what a page should rank for, planning content, or when someone asks about backlinks. It is built from `docs/a11studio.md` (positioning, §18 acquisition) and real GSC/GA4 data (2026-11-24 baseline below). Refresh the baseline with the MCPs before acting on it.

## Baseline (2026-11-24)

- **Google:** 63 impressions and 15 clicks in 90 days, almost all for the brand (`a11studio`, avg position 3.1; also `a11 studio`, `a1 studio`, `studio a11`). Indexed: homepage only.
- **Catalogue:** 1 share-ready artist (the founder), 3 portfolios, 3 collections, ~112 media pages, each × 3 locales.
- **Visitors (GA4, 90 days):** direct 47, Instagram 31+7+2, Reddit 20+2, Facebook 14, LinkedIn 10, Google organic 5. Social and communities bring far more visitors than search today.
- **Old dev URLs:** dev.a11studio.com got impressions until 2026-08-12. It is now `noindex` and unreachable, and those URLs are dropping out of the index.

## Keyword strategy: in this order, and don't skip ahead

1. **The brand** (`a11studio`, `a11 studio`): win position 1. It comes from the same name, bio and link used everywhere (`docs/seo/YOU.md`) and from mentions on other sites. Position 3 for your own brand means Google isn't yet sure the site is the A11STUDIO.
2. **Artists' own names.** `{artist name}`, `{artist name} photographer`, `{name} {specialty} {city}`. This is the platform's real SEO engine: every artist brings their own searches and their own links (Instagram bio, email signature, clients' sites). Only an artist's page can rank for their name, so the work is getting artists to publish complete profiles and link to them.
3. **Long-tail Spanish and Portuguese searches for the product.** Weaker competition than English, and the founder is in Spain:
   `portafolio de fotografía gratis`, `crear portafolio fotógrafo`, `portafolio online para fotógrafos`, `portfólio de fotografia grátis`, `como fazer um portfólio de fotografia`.
4. **Niche intent in English**, where big site builders don't bother:
   `portfolio for drone photographers`, `aerial photography portfolio`, `photography portfolio without a website`, `behance alternative for photographers`, `free photography portfolio no credit card`.
5. **Client searches, later** (`fotógrafo de bodas A Coruña`, `drone photographer Galicia`): this needs many real local artists first (see the directory gate and the growth features in `docs/seo/CLAUDE.md`).

**Don't chase:** `photography portfolio website`, `portfolio website`, `make a portfolio`. Squarespace, Format, Pixpa, Adobe Portfolio and Wix own them, with years of links behind them.

Check volumes in Google Keyword Planner or a paid tool before writing a page. Don't make up numbers. GSC impressions on an indexed page are the ground truth.

## Content that earns links (the Journal, guides)

Pages that attract links are useful to readers and show real experience (E-E-A-T). The founder is an aerial and architecture photographer and filmmaker in A Coruña, which is the angle no generic builder has:

- Practical guides with real experience: "Drone photography rules in Spain (2026)", "How to price aerial photography", "What clients look for in a photography portfolio".
- Journal stories with real work (the format in `docs/a11studio.md` §14), e.g. "Chasing silence in Lanzarote".
- "Photography portfolio examples by specialty", using real A11STUDIO artists (with their permission). It helps them rank and it links into their profiles.

One good guide a month beats ten thin ones. Each guide links to the matching `/for/{discipline}` page and to real artist pages.

## Backlinks: what actually works

From most to least valuable:

1. **Artist links.** Every artist should link their A11STUDIO page from their Instagram bio, Linktree, email signature and client deliveries. Make sharing effortless; that is the product's job.
2. **Founder-led press and local angle.** "A Coruña photographer builds a portfolio platform for artists": pitch it to Galician and Spanish press, photography blogs and podcasts. One real local news link is worth more than 50 directory links.
3. **Communities, done properly.** Reddit already brings more visitors than Google. Answer real questions in r/AskPhotography, r/drones, r/photography and Spanish/Portuguese photography groups; link only when it truly answers the question. Reddit links are nofollow, but AI answers draw on Reddit heavily.
4. **Entity listings** (they make the brand recognisable; the links themselves are weak): Product Hunt, Crunchbase, AlternativeTo ("Behance alternative"), SaaSHub, BetaList, Indie Hackers, G2/Capterra, and a Wikidata item once you have one independent source to cite.
5. **Partnerships** (`docs/a11studio.md` §18): photography schools, associations and drone-pilot courses. Resource pages for students ("free portfolio for our students") are real, relevant links.
6. **Being listed:** ask "best portfolio sites / Behance alternatives" articles to include A11STUDIO, with a short pitch and a demo profile.

**Never:** buy links or "mention packages", PBNs, link exchanges, mass guest posts, fake reviews, extra domains (also in `YOU.md`). The risk is a manual action on a domain that has no authority to spare.

## Timelines to expect

- A brand at position 1: weeks, once the profiles and listings are consistent.
- Long-tail pages: 3–6 months on a new domain, and only after some links exist.
- Thin pages (single photos with no text) won't be indexed whatever the timeline. Fix the content, or keep them out of the index.
